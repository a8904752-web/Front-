Foryou | Simple & Elegant Online Shop
Foryou is a high-quality online store page designed to look like a luxury magazine. Instead of looking like a regular messy shop, it feels calm, clean, and easy to use.


🎨 Design Style
We designed the site to look both expensive and modern:
Fonts: We use a mix of two fonts—one that is very easy to read for most text, and a fancy "italic" style for special headings.
Colors: The background is a soft "Linen" white (which is easier on the eyes than pure white), with deep black and a bright purple for buttons and important details.
Layout: The top of the page is bold and artistic, while the categories are organized in a "Bento Box" style grid (like a neat tray of different sized boxes).
Feel: Everything moves smoothly. When you hover over a product, it gently grows or changes to let you know it's interactive.



🚀 How It’s Built
Tailwind CSS: A tool that helps us style the page quickly and keep the design consistent.
Google Fonts: High-quality text styles that load directly from the web.
Custom Icons: Simple, sharp graphics that look great on any screen size.
Works Everywhere: The layout automatically changes to look perfect on phones, tablets, and large computer screens.



🛠️ Key Features
Magazine-Style Header: A big, bold opening that grabs your attention.
Clean Category Grid: A neat way to show off different types of items like clothes or home goods.
Smart Product Cards: Hovering over an item shows a "Quick Add" button for easy shopping.
See-Through Menu: The top menu stays visible as you scroll but is slightly see-through so it doesn't block the view.
Brand Story: A dark-themed section that tells customers why the brand cares about quality.
